# Site Purpose & Content Summary

## Core Purpose
Landing page for a Discord AI companion bot called "dumfack". The bot is designed to provide natural, human-like chat interactions across multiple customizable personalities without feeling like a standard corporate chatbot.

## Main Copy & Value Proposition
- Category Tag: // discord ai companions
- Main Heading: "it talks like an actual person"
- Subtitle: "multiple personalities, natural pacing, real conversations, not another corporate chatbot."
- Key Metrics:
  - 25+ personalities
  - 5,000+ messages sent
  - 26 servers active

## Interactive Feature Spotlight: "pick your flavor"
Demonstrates personality response variations to the exact same prompt: "hey, how's your day going?"
- Main: Cheerful and enthusiastic response with kaomojis.
- Tsundere: Classic tsundere persona (defensive, reluctant, subtle care).
- Shy: Hesitant, stuttered phrasing with action brackets (*hides face behind hands*).

## Key Features Listed
1. Message Splitting: Sends short, natural chunks rather than massive walls of text.
2. Swappable Personalities: Switch between over 25 personalities (founders, energetic, chill and more) on the fly.
3. Persistent Context: Remembers conversation history across ongoing interactions.
4. Automatic Memory: Auto-saves user details (nicknames, hobbies, preferences).
5. Moderator Priority: Channel restrictions, whitelisting/blacklisting, and temporary mute controls for mods.
6. Reply Regeneration: Option to regenerate/redo unwanted bot responses.

## Call to Action (CTA)
- Live Demo: No signup required to test the bot live on the site.
- Invite Link: Allows users to add the bot directly to their Discord server. (Dumfack#0473 / https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1510277216242766084)
